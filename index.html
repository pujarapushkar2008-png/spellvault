Advanced Cryptographic Info & Security

🛡️ Privacy Notice

Data is stored only on this physical device in your browser's IndexedDB, tied to http://localhost:5173.

Without App Lock, anyone who can open this app can read the unlocked passwords. Use per-app PIN locks or the Master App Lock above for full encryption.

Clearing browser cookies and storage will wipe local data. Export periodic backups to keep your passwords safe.
